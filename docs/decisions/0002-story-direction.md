# ADR 0002: Story direction

**Status:** Accepted (Emad, 2026-10-16)  
**Date:** 2026-10-16

## Context

The project needs a coherent narrative frame for dungeons, classes, and moral stakes beyond a generic Zelda-like prototype.

## Decision

- **Working title:** *Ashfrost: The Last Warden*
- **Premise:** Dead gods’ corpses are **rune temples** holding back endless winter; temples are **going dark**. Player is an **exiled warden** traveling with his **teenage daughter** (absorbing a dead god’s power — hide vs. grow is a father–daughter arc).
- **Tone:** Skyrim (north, runes, mountains) + Witcher (grey contracts) + God of War (parent–child inside myth).
- **Loop:** Temple = puzzle dungeon + **contract** → **kill or spare guardian** → world reacts (full system later).
- **Classes:** God legacies — **warrior**, **rune-reader**, **hunter** (warrior only in V1 slice).
- **V1 content slice:** One temple — **Tomb of the First God** — warrior placeholder.

Detail lives in `docs/GDD.md`; post-V1 systems listed in `docs/backlog.md`.

## Consequences

- Prototype scope anchors on one temple and warrior class; narrative systems (daughter, moral flags, extra temples/classes) are explicitly backlog.
- Open lore beats (broken oath, which god, ending) remain **TBD** until writing passes.
